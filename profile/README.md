## 📊 Why this matters

<p align="center">
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/E--Waste%2FYear-~62M_Tonnes-critical?style=for-the-badge" />
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Jobs%20Needing%20Digital%20Skills-92%25-0077B6?style=for-the-badge" />
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/NC%20Residents%20in%20Poverty-1%20in%208-F4A300?style=for-the-badge" />
</p>

<details>
<summary><b>More on the problem we're solving</b></summary>
<br>

The world generates a record breaking amount of e-waste every year, most of it never recycled. That tech leaks toxic chemicals into soil and water as it breaks down in landfills.

At the same time, 92% of jobs now require some level of digital skill. Without a working computer and the skills to use it, capable people are locked out of the modern workforce. In North Carolina, 1 in 8 residents live below the poverty line. We put rescued technology directly into their hands.

</details>

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Our team

TerraByte Green runs on a small, working structure rather than a large staff. Everyone on the team is hands on in the mission.

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Board of Directors** | Sets strategy and governance for the nonprofit |
| **Founder & CEO** | Leads day to day operations, programs, and the professional services that fund them |
| **Program Mentors** | Run our Tech Mentorship Program, Senior Tech Cafes, Youth Repair Workshops, and AI literacy training, and guide interns |
| **Interns & Volunteers** | Work directly on programs, refurbishment, digital skills and AI training, and IT asset decommissioning |

Interns get individual project space, mentor check ins over Discord or Google Meet, and the work they build stays open for them to use after they leave. We are mission driven, not proprietary about the tools we build.
